# Purli-s-WebOS
Tiana's PalaceOS is a WebOS made with Princess Tiana in mind, but it also has a splash of Purli.

<img width="1424" height="806" alt="Screenshot 2026-09-05 at 18 28 05" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9b077bc8-a487-4b8d-a5a6-112bf663bf8c" />

Try the website: https://purlimurlikas-maker.github.io/Purli-s-WebOS/

To use it, just open the link and test the different features.

The WebOS has two apps: an app with Tiana's famous recipes (you can try them out in real life!), and a settings app where you can change the background of the WebOS. There are links to my GitHub and website, and a website where you can read the story of Tiana. The WebOS has a working clock, pictures/gifs, quotes, and a Beyoncé album. 
Also, to move the Welcome window, you can use the picture of Ray, and drag it around the screen.

Acknowledgements: I got help from people in Hack Club, I used the guide https://jams.hackclub.com/batch/webOS, and when something wasn't working, or I didn't know what to do, I used AI to help me or fix my problems.
